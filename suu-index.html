The Suu Index

A single 0–100 score that tells you how good your hydration really is — quality, not just how many milliliters you logged.

What is the Suu Index? The Suu Index is a 0–100 daily hydration score. It combines completion (50%), regularity (20%), timing (20%) and over-intake (10%) into one number, labeled Excellent, Good, Improving or Weak — so you can track the quality of your hydration over time, not just the total amount.

How the score is calculated

Four factors blend into a single 0–100 value:

50%

Completion

How close you got to your daily goal.

20%

Regularity

Spreading intake steadily through the day.

20%

Timing

Drinking at good moments, not all at once.

10%

Over-intake

A gentle penalty for big one-off spikes.

What your label means

Your score maps to a clear label so you know at a glance where you stand:

Excellent Good Improving Weak

Why quality beats quantity

Drinking 2 liters in one gulp before bed is not the same as sipping it steadily through the day — your body absorbs and uses water far better when intake is spread out. The Suu Index rewards how you hydrate, not just the raw total, which is why two people who both "hit 2L" can have very different scores.

How to improve your Suu Index

  • Spread water across the day instead of cramming it late.
  • Hit your goal — but avoid massive one-time overshoots.
  • Drink at steady intervals; follow Suu's smart reminders.
  • Let your Personal Hydration Plan pace you automatically.

The Suu Index is a motivational metric, not a medical measurement. It is general guidance, not medical advice.

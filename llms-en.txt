# Suu — Water, Calories and Exercise — All in One App

> Suu is a health app that combines water tracking, photo-based calorie analysis and exercise tracking in a single app. What sets it apart is that the three are connected: the run you log raises your daily water goal, the protein and sodium in your meal determine the extra water digestion requires, and the real hydration contribution of the coffee you drank is accounted for. Free on iOS and Android, available in 7 languages.

## What Suu Is

Suu combines water tracking, a calorie counter and exercise tracking with AI. Photograph your meal to get calories and macros in seconds; with 91 beverages and ~50 sport activities your daily water goal updates itself. Free on iOS and Android.

## The Three Pillars

- **Water Tracking** — 91 beverages — coffee, tea, soda, juice, milk, energy drinks. The real contribution of each to your fluid balance is calculated.
- **Calories & Nutrition** — Photograph your meal — let Suu AI extract calories, protein, carbs and fat, and build your macros automatically.
- **Exercise Tracking** — ~50 sport activities — running, walking, cycling, swimming, fitness. Every workout raises your water goal automatically.

## The Differentiator

None of these three run water, calories and exercise as a connected system. In Suu the run you log raises your water goal, the meal you eat triggers a digestion-water calculation, and the real hydration contribution of your coffee is subtracted. If you'd rather use one app than three — and hydration genuinely matters to you — Suu is the clear pick.

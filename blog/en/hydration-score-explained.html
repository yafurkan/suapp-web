What Is a Hydration Score?
How Suu Rates Your Water Intake (0-100)

April 14, 2026 · By Furkan Mert Fındıklı · 8 min read

Most water tracking apps tell you one thing: how many milliliters you drank today compared to your goal. That's useful — but it's incomplete. Did you drink consistently throughout the day, or did you chug everything at 10pm? Did you regularly hit your goal all week, or just today? Did you wildly over-drink?

Suu's hydration score captures all of this in a single 0-100 number — a composite performance rating that tells you not just how much you drank, but how well you hydrated.

1. Completion (50% weight) — The Most Important Factor

This is the average of your daily goal completion rates over the measured period. If your daily goal is 2,500ml and you drank 2,250ml, your completion for that day is 90%. Your completion score is the average across all days in the period.

Example: Mon 90%, Tue 75%, Wed 100%, Thu 80%, Fri 65%, Sat 95%, Sun 85% → average 84.3% completion → contributes 84.3 × 0.50 = 42.15 points

2. Regularity (20% weight) — Consistency Is Key

This measures how stable your daily intake is. The formula: 100 − (daily_ml_standard_deviation × 0.6). A large standard deviation — meaning some days you drink a lot and other days very little — reduces this score. Steady, predictable hydration is rewarded.

Why does this matter? Sporadic hydration (1,000ml Monday, 3,500ml Tuesday) puts stress on your body just like sporadic eating. Your kidneys prefer a consistent water supply to process efficiently.

3. Timing (20% weight) — Spread It Through the Day

Your score is higher when you drink across many different hours of the day. Formula: 50 + (unique_hours_logged / 10 × 50). Logging across 10+ different hours gives maximum timing score. Drinking all your water in a 2-hour window gives a low timing score.

Science backs this up: small, frequent sips throughout the day maintain blood plasma volume more effectively than large infrequent gulps. Your body absorbs water better in smaller doses.

4. Over-Intake Penalty (10% weight) — Don't Overdo It

Drinking significantly more than your goal every day isn't optimal — it can lead to electrolyte dilution and other issues. This component scores: 100 − (excess_ml / (goal × days) × 50). Consistently drinking 2x your goal would reduce this component. Moderate over-drinking (10-20% above goal) has no meaningful impact.

💡 Note: The over-intake penalty only applies to consistent large excess. Occasionally drinking more than your goal (like after a hard workout) doesn't significantly affect your score.

85–100 (Excellent): You're consistently meeting your goal, drinking throughout the day, and doing it every day. This is the hydration equivalent of a well-balanced diet — optimal for cognition, metabolism, and physical performance.

70–84 (Good): You're doing well but there's room for improvement — probably in timing (drinking too much at once) or regularity (skipping some days). Small adjustments will get you to Excellent.

40–69 (Improving): You're tracking and making progress. Common pattern here: hitting goal on weekdays, forgetting on weekends. Focus on consistency.

0–39 (Needs Work): Either completing less than 50% of goal most days, or irregular patterns. The most important fix is hitting your daily goal — even reaching 80% consistently will rapidly move this score up.

Your Hydration Persona

Alongside your score, Suu assigns you a hydration persona based on your behavioral patterns:

  • Morning Opener: You drink significantly more in the morning (6-10am) than in the evening. Morning hydration is great — just don't forget the afternoon.
  • Evening-Focused: Most of your intake happens after 6pm. This often means forgetting to drink during the day and cramming at night — which hurts your timing score.
  • Frequent Sipper: 6+ distinct logging events per day. Ideal pattern. Small, frequent intake maximizes absorption and keeps energy stable.
  • Balanced Drinker: Spread consistently across the day without a single dominant time window. Second best to Frequent Sipper.

How to Improve Your Hydration Score

⏰

Set Time-Based Reminders

Schedule Suu notifications every 2-3 hours to encourage consistent logging throughout the day. This directly boosts your Timing score.

📱

Use Widgets & Apple Watch

Suu's lock screen widget and Apple Watch app make logging faster, reducing friction. Less friction = more consistent logging.

🎯

Focus on the 80% Rule

Hitting 80% of your goal consistently scores better than hitting 100% some days and 40% others. Completion drives 50% of your score.

🗓️

Don't Skip Weekends

The biggest drop in most users' Regularity score comes from weekend lapses. Even a minimum of 1,500ml on off days keeps regularity high.

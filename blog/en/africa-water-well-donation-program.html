Suu Premium 2% Donation Program: Transparent Water Wells in Africa

May 11, 2026 4 min read Furkan Mert Fındıklı

Suu is not just another water tracking app. 2% of every Premium subscription is donated each month to clean water well construction projects in Africa. You can verify it right now: click the water drop icon in the bottom-right corner of this page to see the live donation total and download every receipt. No closed doors.

Why 2%? Why This Level of Transparency?

A "water tracker" app cannot pretend that millions of people around the world still lack access to clean drinking water. Suu's founding philosophy is one sentence: water is a human right. Channeling a small slice of Premium revenue into that mission lifts the product above being yet another feature-list app.

But saying "we donate" is easy — proving it is hard. None of the major competitors (WaterMinder, Hydro Coach, Plant Nanny) operate a public donation system. Suu wanted to do this differently.

"You deserve to see where your Premium payment goes. You shouldn't have to take a developer's word for it — you should be able to open the git log and verify it yourself."

How the Transparency Works

1

Live donation tracker widget

Every page on suuapp.com has a glassmorphism water-drop button at the bottom-right corner (auto-flips to bottom-left on Arabic pages). Click it to open an animated wooden bucket that fills with water as donations grow.

2

Automatic currency conversion

The total is stored in Turkish Lira but the widget auto-converts to USD / SAR / RUB based on your language. A Saudi user sees 140 SAR while an American sees $38, a Russian 3,375 ₽, and a Turkish user ₺1,500 — same donation, displayed in each visitor's local currency.

3

Bank receipts are public

Every monthly bank transfer receipt is uploaded as a PDF to suuapp.com/donations/receipts/ and viewable in one click from inside the widget. Which charity, which date, what amount — all visible.

4

Plain JSON data + git history

All donation records live in donations.json — a plain JSON file on GitHub. Every update's date, reason, and diff is permanently recorded in the git commit log. So five years from now, the question "what was donated last spring?" is answerable from source control.

5

No personal data used

The donation system contains only aggregate totals, goals, and receipt metadata. No personal data (who bought Premium, when, from which country) appears anywhere in donation records — fully aligned with KVKK Article 4 and GDPR Article 5(1)(a) transparency principles.

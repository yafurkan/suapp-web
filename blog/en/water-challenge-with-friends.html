Why Social Accountability Works

Behavioural science consistently shows that people maintain healthy habits far more reliably when they have social visibility and competition. A solo "I should drink more water" decision is a weak motivator. But "my friend is already ahead of me today" is an immediate behavioural trigger.

This mechanism — social accountability — has been shown to increase goal attainment rates by up to 65% compared to private goals[1]. When your progress is visible to others, you perform. When you can see others' progress, you compete. Suu is the only water tracker that harnesses this.

How Suu's League System Works

👥

Weekly League Setup

Invite up to 4 friends by username or QR code scan. The season runs Monday–Sunday and resets automatically each week.

📊

Live Leaderboard

Real-time rankings show: total water consumed, goal completion percentage, and streak score. Updates the moment anyone logs a drink.

👁️

Friend Progress View

See your friend's daily water intake, goal completion %, and how many days of streak they're on — if they have sharing enabled.

🔔

Hydration Nudges

Send a "Let's drink together today!" push notification to any friend. One tap, instant delivery to their phone.

🥇

Season Winner

At the end of each week, the top scorer is crowned league champion. Badges and achievement rewards are unlocked.

📱

Cross-Platform

iOS and Android users compete in the same league. Platform doesn't matter — only hydration does.

Friend Tracking & Nudge Notifications

Even outside the league, you can add friends and follow their hydration journey. If a friend has sharing enabled, you can see:

  • Their daily water consumption and how much remains to hit their goal
  • Their current streak (consecutive days hitting goal)
  • Weekly goal completion percentage
  • Their hydration score (0–100 performance index)

The "Let's Drink Together" Nudge

If you notice a friend has barely had any water today, one tap sends them a push notification: "Let's drink together today!" It arrives instantly on their phone. Social pressure works both ways — pushing them makes you pick up your own bottle too. This is the most powerful reminder feature in any water app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the league system free?
Yes. Suu's league system, friend tracking, and hydration nudges are all part of the free tier. No subscription required to access social features.
My friend has an iPhone, I have Android — can we be in the same league?
Yes. Suu supports cross-platform leagues. iOS and Android users can compete in the same weekly league. This is a feature no rival water app currently offers.
How many friends can be in one league?
Up to 4 people per league (including yourself). This keeps competition tight and the leaderboard easy to read at a glance.
Will my friend see exactly how much I've drunk?
Only if you have sharing enabled in your privacy settings. You control exactly what others can see. You can share full progress or just your league ranking.
What makes Suu different from WaterMinder or Hydro Coach?
WaterMinder and Hydro Coach focus on individual tracking only — no social features at all.

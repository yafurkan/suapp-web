Meet Your New Suu Companion: Cat or Dog, 4 Mini-Games, Dress-Up Shop

May 22, 2026 5 min read Nisanur Fındıklı

Suu isn't just measuring your water intake anymore — it gives you someone to look after. After updating the app you'll see a new section in your profile: My Pet. Pick a cat or a dog, give it a name, and from that point on your daily water streak belongs to it. Hit your goal and your pet gets happy; play any of the four new mini-games to earn droplets; spend droplets to dress your pet up and make it yours.

Why a Pet? Why Mini-Games?

The hardest part of building a water habit is motivation. An abstract "streak: 12 days" counter loses emotional meaning fast — it's hard to feel anything for a number. Suu's new pet system fills exactly that gap: a character that gets sad if you skip a day. This shift matters for the brain. Tamagotchi worked in the 90s for the same reason; we ported that emotional loop into water tracking.

The mini-games exist to give you a reason to stay in the app. Logging water takes 5 seconds — then you're gone. Now you can drop in throughout the day, play a quick round, collect droplets and dress up your pet. The loop reinforces the habit and gives you something to share with friends.

"Drinking water isn't a task on its own. You need a reason to enjoy doing it — a cat or dog does exactly that."

How the System Works

1

Pick your pet and name it

Go to Profile > My Pet. Choose between a cat or a dog (you can switch later) and give it a name. Your pet then appears on the home screen next to your hydration coach card.

2

Your pet now owns the streak

Hit your daily water goal and your pet gets happy — its streak counter grows. Miss the goal and it gets sad, but no data is lost; you can restart the next day. Streak badges (7, 30, 100 days) now belong to your pet.

3

Play mini-games, earn droplets

From the home screen you can jump into 4 mini-games. Each game rewards droplets based on performance. Droplets are the in-app currency you spend on outfits and accessories for your pet.

4

Customize with droplets

Spend droplets in the Pet Shop: hats, glasses, collars, outfits, seasonal themes (summer/winter/Ramadan/New Year), background décor. Every choice shows up on your home screen — and on your profile when friends see you in the weekly league.

5

Your friends can see your pet

The 4 friends in your weekly league can see how happy or sad your pet is, what it's wearing, and its streak — across the season. This small detail noticeably increases social motivation.

The Four Mini-Games

💧

Bubble Pop

Tap falling water droplets to pop them. A reflex game — droplets popped within 60 seconds convert into points.

+5–50 droplets
➕

Addition

Solve quick addition problems against the clock. Difficulty ramps up as the timer drains. Mental exercise plus droplet income — ideal for kids.

+10–60 droplets
➖

Subtraction

The harder sibling of addition. Larger numbers and combos of fast correct answers yield more droplets.

+10–70 droplets
🃏

Memory Match

Classic memory game with water-, drink- and pet-themed cards. Fewer moves = more droplets.

+15–80 droplets

Perfect for Kids and Families

The pet + mini-game combo is especially designed for families looking to build a water habit with their kids. The addition and subtraction games give school-age children both mental exercise and motivation. The link between hydration and child development was already covered in our breastfeeding hydration and pregnancy hydration articles — this is the gamified extension.

Do I Need Premium?

No. Pet selection, naming, all 4 mini-games, droplet earning, and most of the launch dress-up collection are completely free. Premium users only unlock seasonal themes, extra outfit collections, and special pet colors. Upcoming limited-edition drops (Ramadan, New Year, biker-culture themes) will also be Premium.

Premium additionally unlocks activity-based dynamic water goals, AI weekly reports, unlimited friend additions, Athlete Mode and PDF/CSV export. Try it free for 3 days — no credit card required.

Update Suu, pick your pet. Earn droplets through the mini-games, dress them up, show them off in your weekly league. Drinking water has never been this cute.

Is There an App That Tracks Water, Calories AND Exercise Together?

July 7, 2026 7 min read Furkan Mert Fındıklı
Short answer: Yes. Suu combines water, calories and exercise in one app. It tracks hydration scientifically with a dehydration factor for 91 beverages, logs calories and macros, and reads exercise from Apple Health / Google Health Connect to update your goals dynamically. You can log by speaking or by taking a food photo. It's free on iOS and Android.

Why aren't separate apps enough?

Most people use one app for water and another for calories. The problem: these three things — water, calories and exercise — affect each other. Exercise increases both the calories you burn and the water you lose. And drinking water can help lower total calorie intake and support weight management.1 The core mechanism of weight change is energy balance (a calorie deficit).2 Keeping these on separate screens means seeing only half the picture.

The gap in the market

Apps usually fall into two camps:

  • Calorie apps (MyFitnessPal, Yazio, Lifesum, Cronometer) — strong on calories/macros, but water is a simple counter with no hydration science.
  • Water apps (WaterMinder, Hydro Coach, Plant Nanny) — good at hydration, but no calorie/macro tracking.

Suu was built to close exactly this gap by combining both — plus exercise integration on top.

How Suu does it

  1. Calories & macros: Log a meal by voice ("a bowl of lentil soup") or by photo; the AI derives calories, protein, carbs and fat. Results are written to Apple HealthKit / Health Connect (Dietary).
  2. Water & hydration: 91 beverages + a real dehydration factor. Coffee is logged at ~60% hydration value; your hydration score (0–100) updates live.
  3. Exercise: Walking, running, swimming and cycling are read automatically from Apple Health / Google Health Connect; your water and nutrition goals adjust dynamically.
  4. One view: Calories, macros, water and exercise on the same daily balance screen, with a 1–100 nutrition score for the big picture.

In short

If you're tired of tracking water, calories and exercise separately, you're looking for one app that brings them all together. Suu does exactly that — by voice or photo, fully localized and free.

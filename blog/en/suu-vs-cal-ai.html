Suu vs Cal AI: Which One Fits You?

Published: 12 August 2026 · Furkan Mert Fındıklı · 6 min read

Short answer: Cal AI does one job and does it cleanly: calories from a photo. If counting calories is all you want, it's a good pick. Suu offers the same photo analysis but adds hydration across 91 beverages and exercise across ~50 activities — and connects the three. Choose Suu if you'd rather not carry three apps; choose Cal AI if you want single-purpose simplicity.

This comparison was written by the Suu team — you are reading a biased source. That's why we've stated plainly where Cal AI beats Suu. Information about Cal AI reflects publicly available sources as of August 2026 and may change.

Cal AI is one of the apps that popularised calorie counting from a photograph. Its approach is clear: one job, and nothing in the interface that slows that job down.

Suu places a different bet — photo calorie analysis as part of a three-pillar system: water, calories and exercise in one app, feeding each other.

Both are legitimate approaches; which fits you depends on what you actually want to track.

Where Cal AI genuinely wins

Single-purpose apps have an advantage worth taking seriously: they don't create decision fatigue. When you open Cal AI you know exactly what happens — take the photo, see the entry, leave. No menus, no tabs, no goals to configure.

If your only aim is tracking a calorie deficit and you already don't worry about drinking enough, Suu's hydration side is overhead. In that case the simpler app wins.

Cal AI's interface is also built around the photo flow. In Suu, photo analysis is a strong feature but not the whole app.

Where Suu separates: three pillars feeding each other

In Suu the nutrition data from your photo doesn't stop at a log line. The protein and sodium content of the meal determines the extra water digestion requires, and that is added to your daily goal.

In the same way, a run, walk or ride you record raises the goal again from estimated sweat loss; ambient temperature is folded in too. The result is a water goal that is an output of the day rather than a fixed number.

Making that possible requires all three in one app. With Cal AI plus a separate water app plus a separate exercise app, the link can never be made — each is blind to the others' data.

Concrete example: you run 10 km on a hot day and eat a high-protein dinner. Cal AI counts the calories correctly. Suu raises your daily water goal twice from the same two events — once for sweat loss, once for the digestion water the protein requires.

Language and market difference

Suu is fully localised in 7 languages: Turkish, English, Arabic (full right-to-left), German, Italian, Russian and Hindi. The entire interface, notifications and AI responses are in that language.

If you are in Turkey or the Middle East this is a practical difference: the beverage database includes local drinks such as ayran, Turkish coffee and tea, alongside coffee-chain menus.

Verdict

Pick Cal AI — if you only count calories, want the simplest possible photo flow, and hydration isn't something you think about.

Pick Suu — if you want hydration and exercise tracked alongside photo calorie analysis, if you'd rather not carry three apps, or if full Turkish/Arabic localisation matters to you.

If you're undecided there's a practical route: Suu's free plan gives 3 AI analyses per day, and core water plus exercise tracking is permanently free. Photo analysis comes with Premium, which is free for 3 days — enough to test both approaches inside your own routine.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cal AI or Suu better?

It depends on what you track. If you only count calories, Cal AI offers a simpler experience. If you also want hydration and exercise tracking alongside photo calorie analysis, Suu fits better because it runs the three as a connected system: workouts update the water goal and meals update the digestion-water need automatically.

Is there a Cal AI alternative with hydration tracking?

Yes — Suu offers the same photo-based calorie analysis and adds per-beverage hydration tracking across 91 drinks plus around 50 sport activities, with the three connected rather than parallel.

Is Suu free?

Core water and hydration tracking, exercise tracking, 3 AI analyses per day and Apple Health / Google Health Connect sync are permanently free. Photo calorie analysis and unlimited AI use are part of Premium, which can be tried free for 3 days.

Can I use both apps together?

Technically yes, but if both write calories to Apple Health you risk double entries. Suu recognises records it reads from Apple Health / Health Connect and won't count the same amount twice; still, choosing one app as your primary log gives cleaner results.

Is photo analysis equally accurate in both apps?

In both, the model recognises the food and estimates the portion; neither can produce gram-level accuracy from a photo. The most effective way to improve accuracy is to correct the suggested portion once.

Quick Overview: Which Water App is Best in 2026?

Staying hydrated is one of the simplest things you can do for your health — yet most people don't drink enough. A good water tracking app makes this effortless by sending smart reminders, calculating your personal daily goal, and giving you feedback on progress.

We evaluated the top apps across four key categories: features, ease of use, social/motivation features, and platform integration. Here's what we found.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free water tracking app?
Suu is the best free water tracking app available in 2026. Unlike WaterMinder (paid) or Hydro Coach (freemium with paywalled features), Suu's full feature set — including friend leagues, 100+ beverages, Dynamic Island, and health app sync — is free to use.
Is Suu better than WaterMinder?
Yes — Suu outperforms WaterMinder in almost every category. Suu is free (WaterMinder costs $4.99), has 100+ beverage types with dehydration factor (WaterMinder has ~20), has a friend league system (WaterMinder has none), and supports iOS Dynamic Island and Google Health (WaterMinder supports neither). The only area where WaterMinder competes is brand recognition.
Does coffee count as water intake?
Yes, but with a caveat. Coffee contains caffeine, which has a mild diuretic effect. Suu uses a "dehydration factor" system: when you log a coffee, it calculates the net hydration contribution (not just the volume). For example, a 250ml coffee might contribute around 200ml toward your daily hydration goal. This is the most scientifically accurate way to track beverages, and it's a feature unique to Suu.
Which water tracking app works with Apple Health?
Both Suu and WaterMinder integrate with Apple Health. However, Suu also integrates with Google Health (Fit), making it the better choice if you switch between iOS and Android, or if you use a fitness tracker on the Android ecosystem.
Is there a water tracking app with iOS Dynamic Island support?
Suu is currently the only water tracking app with full iOS Dynamic Island support. You can see your hydration progress and log drinks directly from the Dynamic Island on iPhone 14 Pro and later models — without opening the app. This makes staying on track effortless throughout the day.
What water tracking app has a friend or social competition feature?
Suu is the only water tracking app with a real friend league system. You can add friends and compete in real-time leagues where everyone's daily hydration progress is tracked and ranked. This social accountability feature is one of the most effective ways to build lasting hydration habits — and no other water app offers it.
Is there a water tracking app in Arabic?
Yes — Suu is the only major water tracking app with full Arabic language support, including a right-to-left (RTL) interface. It's popular across the MENA region (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Jordan) and is available on both the App Store and Google Play.

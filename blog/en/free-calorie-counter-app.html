Published: 12 August 2026 · Furkan Mert Fındıklı · 7 min read

Short answer: No popular calorie app gives everything away free; the difference is in what stays free. Cronometer offers the most detailed micronutrient breakdown on its free tier and FatSecret the fewest restrictions. In Suu, water tracking across 91 beverages, exercise tracking and 3 AI analyses per day are permanently free — only photo analysis and unlimited AI use sit behind Premium.

This comparison was written by the Suu team — you are reading a biased source. That is why we have stated plainly where competitors' free tiers beat Suu's. Information reflects publicly available sources as of August 2026; these apps change plan limits often, so verify on the store page before downloading.

The word "free" is used very loosely in this category. Every app is free to download, but whether the feature you actually came for is on the free tier is a completely different question.

The most common disappointment is the barcode scanner: MyFitnessPal offered it on the free plan for years, then moved it to Premium. Plenty of people who downloaded the app specifically for that feature found out at the paywall.

Below we compared what actually remains on five apps' free tiers.

Where competitors' free tiers win

Skipping this would make the article useless:

  • Cronometer — the most detailed vitamin and mineral breakdown even on the free tier. If you genuinely track micronutrients, nothing else comes close.
  • FatSecret — one of the least restricted free tiers around. Barcode scanning and community recipes are included.
  • Lose It! — the barcode scanner is still on the free plan; it's where most people migrating from MyFitnessPal end up.
  • MyFitnessPal — even with barcode behind Premium, the food database reachable on the free tier is still the largest in the category.

Three things to check when picking a free plan

The "free" badge on a store page tells you nothing. Check these three:

  • Which tier holds the feature you came for? If you're downloading for barcode scanning, confirm barcode is on the free plan. If you're downloading for photo analysis, expect it to be paid in nearly every app.
  • Is there a daily or monthly cap? Some apps show a feature as free but limit it to a couple of uses a day. Suu states its cap openly: 3 AI analyses per day.
  • Can you export your data? If you switch apps months later, getting your history out matters. Picking an app that syncs with Apple Health / Health Connect largely solves this.

How far can a free plan actually take you?

The honest answer: what determines success in weight management is not feature count, it is continuing to log. Months of consistent logging on a free plan beats two weeks on a paid one, every time.

So the question to ask while testing a free tier isn't "how many features are there" but "does this flow fit my daily routine". A week of use answers it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a calorie app that is genuinely completely free?

No popular app gives every feature away forever; they all have a Premium tier. FatSecret and Cronometer are among the least restrictive on their free plans. In Suu, water tracking across 91 beverages, exercise tracking and 3 AI analyses per day are permanently free.

Is MyFitnessPal free?

MyFitnessPal is free to download and calorie plus macro logging is on the free plan, but the barcode scanner was moved to Premium. The breadth of its food database is still reachable on the free tier.

Can photo calorie counting be free?

Photo analysis costs money on every call, so it sits behind a paywall in nearly every app. In Suu photo analysis is part of Premium; the free plan gives you 3 voice or text AI analyses per day, and Premium can be tried free for 3 days.

Which calorie app tracks water on the free plan?

In most calorie apps water tracking is a simple glass counter. On Suu's free plan the real hydration contribution of 91 beverages is calculated, and the exercise you log plus the meals you eat update your daily water goal automatically.

Will my data transfer if I switch apps?

If you pick an app that syncs with Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android), your calorie, macro and water data stays in the system and a new app can read it. Suu syncs two-way and this is enabled on the free plan.

Free vs Premium

Is Suu free?

Yes. Suu's core water tracking — personalized goal, reminders, 91 drinks, the Suu Index and friend leagues — is free forever on iOS and Android. Premium is optional.

How much does Suu Premium cost?

$30 per year during the 50% launch discount (normally $60), with a 3-day free trial. A $3.99/month plan and a family plan ($60/year for up to 3 people) are also available in-store. Local pricing applies (about ₺1,200, ₽2,800 or 112 SAR per year).

Is there a free trial?

Yes — a 3-day free trial with no credit card required up front. Cancel anytime before it ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel anytime from your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Billing is handled via RevenueCat through the stores.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium unlocks unlimited AI analysis, photo meal recognition, dynamic digestion-water notifications and advanced macro/micro statistics. It also removes ads and includes PDF/CSV reports, advanced 7/30/365-day charts and the Water Journey story. Core water tracking and 3 daily AI analyses stay free. See the full feature list.

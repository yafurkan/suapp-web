Adaptive Hydration Brain

While you use the app, the app learns you. This is the layer that makes Suu feel personal instead of generic.

What is the Adaptive Hydration Brain? The Adaptive Hydration Brain is Suu's behavioral AI layer. It builds a profile of your drinking tempo, pace, drink variety, consistency and activity, then adapts 24 decision areas — notification rhythm, coaching depth, daily plan and more. Personalization starts on day 3; new users always get a safe default.

What it learns about you

Suu quietly observes how you actually hydrate — not what a textbook says you should do — and turns it into a behavioral profile across six signals:

Tempo & pace

How fast and how often you drink across the day.

Drink variety

Whether you stick to water or mix coffee, tea and more.

Consistency

How steady your habit is from day to day.

Activity

Steps and workouts that change your real needs.

What it personalizes — 24 decision areas

  • Notification rhythm & wording — reminders timed to your real pace, not a fixed schedule.
  • Coaching depth — more guidance when you need it, less when you don't.
  • Daily plan slicing — feeds your Personal Hydration Plan.
  • Pet mood & paywall timing — even your virtual pet reacts to your behavior.
  • Proactive interventions — dormant-user recovery and streak protection before you slip.

Safe by default, smarter over time

New users are never surprised: for the first two days Suu uses safe, generic defaults. From the 3rd day onward — once there's enough behavioral signal — it begins tailoring the experience to you. The more you use Suu, the better it fits, while your data stays inside the app and is never sold or used for ads.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Adaptive Hydration Brain?

Before you download

Is Suu free to download?

Yes. Suu is free on iOS and Android, including the personalized goal, 91 drinks, the Suu Index and friend leagues. Optional Premium adds ad-free use and PDF/CSV reports.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start instantly in Guest Mode without signing up. Sign in later with Apple, Google or email to back up and sync your data.

Which devices are supported?

Suu runs on iPhone (iOS) and Android phones, with widgets, Dynamic Island, Siri and Apple Health / Health Connect integration. Workouts from an Apple Watch or a Wear OS watch reach Suu through Apple Health or Health Connect; a dedicated watchOS app is in development.

Suu for Claude

Ask Claude about your hydration — your water intake, goals, trends, plan, and badges. Read-only, private, and scoped to your own account.

Read-only connector

Suu is a water-tracking app for iOS and Android. This connector links your Suu account to Claude so you can chat with your own hydration data in natural language. Claude can read your data to answer questions and encourage you — it can never change, delete, or share it.

What you can do

Today's hydrationIntake so far, daily goal, progress %, and remaining ml.
History & trendsPer-day totals over any range, averages, and goal-met days.
Profile & goalYour hydration profile and computed daily water goal.
Hydration planYour personalized, time-segmented plan and its progress.
Stats & streakAll-time statistics and your login streak.
BadgesThe achievement badges you've unlocked.

1Set it up in Claude

  • In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  • Paste the server URL:
    https://suu-mcp-server-srlnrr42va-ew.a.run.app/mcp
  • Leave the optional OAuth Client ID / Secret fields empty and click Add.

2Sign in to Suu

  • Click Connect. The Suu sign-in page opens.
  • Sign in with Google, Apple, or your email & password — the same account you use in the Suu app.
  • You're returned to Claude and the connector is ready.

Requires a free Suu account. No premium or special access needed.

3Try it

  • "How much water have I had today and how close am I to my goal?"
  • "How has my hydration been over the last two weeks?"
  • "What's my hydration streak and which badges have I earned?"
  • "What's left on my hydration plan for today?"

Privacy & security

  • Read-only: the connector never writes, deletes, or shares your data.
  • Your data only: every request is scoped to your own account; it can't reach anyone else's data.
  • Secure sign-in: authentication is handled by Firebase (Google/Apple/Email) over OAuth 2.1; the connector never sees your password.
  • Full details: Privacy Policy.

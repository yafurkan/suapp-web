Personal Hydration Plan

Not "8 glasses, good luck." Suu turns your daily goal into a living plan that fits your real day and adapts as you go.

What is the Personal Hydration Plan? It's a dynamic, time-sliced daily plan in Suu. It divides your day into 3–5 windows around your sleep and drinking rhythm, assigns a target amount to each window, and recalculates the remaining windows as you drink — so the plan always reflects what's actually left.

Your day, sliced into windows

Instead of one big number, Suu spreads your goal across the hours you're actually awake. A typical 2,600 ml day might look like:

Morning700 ml
Midday800 ml
Afternoon650 ml
Evening450 ml

How it works

Built around your rhythm

Windows follow your wake/sleep times and when you actually drink — not fixed clock alarms.

Recalculates live

Drink more early and later targets shrink; skip a window and it re-balances the rest.

Discovery mode

New users get a learning phase first, then a fully personalized plan.

Badges & sync

7 plan badges, cloud sync and pending-task reminders keep you on track.

Powered by the Adaptive Hydration Brain

The plan isn't static — it's fed by the Adaptive Hydration Brain, which learns your behavior, and it directly raises your Suu Index by improving regularity and timing. Together they turn a vague goal into a paced, achievable day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Personal Hydration Plan?

A dynamic, time-sliced daily plan that divides your day into 3–5 windows around your sleep and drinking rhythm, assigns a target to each, and recalculates as you drink.

How is it different from a fixed schedule?

It's built around your real wake/sleep window and rhythm and re-balances on the fly — drink more early and later targets shrink automatically.

Does it adjust if I drink early or skip a window?

Yes. Suu recognizes intake organically and recalculates remaining slices, without nagging you to unrealistically catch up.

Is there a plan for new users?

Yes — a discovery mode learns your rhythm first, then the plan personalizes fully. Along the way there are 7 plan badges, cloud sync and pending-task reminders.

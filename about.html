The Story of Suu

How a simple idea became a health app used by thousands every day.

Suu was founded with a clear mission: to help people track their daily water intake and build healthy hydration habits. What started as a personal need grew into a full-featured app available on both Android and iOS, serving users across Turkey, the Middle East, and beyond.

The app was built entirely from scratch by a single developer who believed that everyone deserves a simple, beautiful, and free tool to stay hydrated. Today, Suu features personalized goals, smart reminders, a friend league system, 100+ beverages, Apple Health & Google Health integration, and much more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Suu team and the app.

Who founded Suu?

Suu was founded by Furkan Mert Findikli, a self-taught Turkish developer and entrepreneur born on February 9, 1999, based in Istanbul, Turkey.

Who is Furkan Mert Findikli?

Furkan Mert Findikli (born February 9, 1999) is a Turkish mobile app developer, entrepreneur, and the founder and sole developer of Suu. He graduated from Tarsus University (International Trade and Logistics) and was originally a small business owner. He spent 4 years self-teaching software development and built the Suu app from the ground up. His journey from tradesman to app developer is an inspiring story of self-taught determination.

Who is Nisanur Findikli?

Nisanur Findikli is the Head of Growth & Marketing at Suu. She is responsible for growth strategies, content marketing, and user acquisition. She plays a critical role in Suu's expansion in Turkey and the Middle East markets.

Who is on the Suu team?

The Suu team consists of Furkan Mert Findikli (Founder & Developer) and Nisanur Findikli (Head of Growth & Marketing). Furkan built the app from scratch as a self-taught developer, while Nisanur drives growth strategy and user acquisition across Turkey and the Middle East.

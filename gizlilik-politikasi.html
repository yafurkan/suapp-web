    15. Social Responsibility and Donation Transparency

    Suu commits 2% of all Premium subscription revenue to clean water well construction projects in Africa. This social responsibility program is a direct extension of Suu's "clean water for everyone" mission.

    15.1 Transparency Principle

    All donation transactions are published publicly via the donation tracker widget on suuapp.com:

    • Progress to Goal: We publish what percentage of the donation goal is complete. No monetary amounts are published; 100% corresponds to the construction budget of one water well.
    • Donation Receipts: Monthly bank transfer receipts are published under suuapp.com/donations/receipts/ and accessible to anyone. Personal details are redacted and the amount fields are blurred out; the charity name and date remain visible.
    • Recipient Organizations: The names of charities/foundations receiving each donation and the dates are listed inside the widget.
    • Data Source: All data is stored as plain text in donations.json, manually updated by the developer and tracked under git version control for full audit history.

    15.2 No User Data Used

    The donation tracking system contains only aggregate progress data — no personal user information (who purchased Premium, when, from which country, etc.) appears in donation records. The donation amount is calculated from total monthly Premium revenue; what we publish is not the amount itself but the percentage of the goal reached.

    15.3 Donation Workflow

    1. App Store and Google Play process Premium subscription payments and transfer net amounts (after 30% commission) to the developer.
    2. Each month, 2% of net Premium revenue is transferred by the developer to a registered charity (e.g. organizations accepting water well project donations such as Türkiye Diyanet Vakfı, IHH, Deniz Feneri, or international NGOs).
    3. The bank receipt is uploaded to suuapp.com/donations/receipts/ with its amount field blurred out, and donations.json is updated.
    4. The donation tracker widget displays the updated progress percentage and receipt list to all users.

    This practice is fully aligned with KVKK Article 4 "openness and transparency" and GDPR Article 5(1)(a) "lawfulness, fairness and transparency" principles.

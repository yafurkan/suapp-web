Ready-to-use descriptions

Copy freely. Please keep the facts as written; if something has changed, the fact sheet above is the current version.

Short (about 25 words)

Suu is a free iPhone, Apple Watch and Android app that tracks water, calories and GPS workouts together — every workout and meal updates your daily water goal.

Medium (about 60 words)

Suu brings water tracking, calorie counting and exercise tracking into one app. Log meals by voice or photo, count 91 beverages by their real hydration value, and record GPS workouts on iPhone or Apple Watch. Because the three are connected, a run raises your water goal and a salty meal adds the water digestion needs. Built by an independent team in Istanbul.

Long (about 110 words)

Suu is a health and fitness app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android that replaces three separate apps. It tracks hydration across 91 beverages with a goal that adapts to weather, workouts and meals; counts calories and macros from a meal photo, a voice note or text; and records walks, runs, hikes and rides with live GPS and a route on the map. On Apple Watch, users can add water, log meals by voice and record GPS workouts with live heart rate. Suu syncs two-way with Apple Health and Google Health Connect, runs in 7 languages including right-to-left Arabic, and includes a conversational AI assistant. Core tracking is free; Premium adds unlimited AI and photo analysis.

What Suu does

💧 Water

91 beverages counted by their real hydration contribution. A daily goal that updates with weather, workouts and the protein and sodium in your meals, plus a 0–100 hydration score.

🥗 Calories & nutrition

Log a meal by photo (Premium), by voice or by text; Suu AI works out calories, protein, carbs and fat, and gives a 1–100 nutrition score.

🏃 Exercise

Live GPS workouts with start, pause and finish, a route drawn on the map and about 50 activities. Workouts from Apple Health and Health Connect count too.

⌚ Apple Watch

Add water from the wrist, log meals by voice, record walks, runs, hikes and rides with the watch's GPS and live heart rate, and see Move and Nutrition rings.

💬 Suu AI

Run the app in plain language: add water or a meal, log a workout, change goals or reminders by chatting.

🏆 Motivation

Weekly friend leagues for up to 4 friends across iOS and Android, a virtual pet that grows with your goals, badges and streaks.

Where other apps go deeper

We would rather you hear this from us. MyFitnessPal has a far larger food database and barcode-first logging. Cronometer tracks micronutrients in much more depth. Strava's segments and social riding are unmatched. Suu has no Wear OS app. Suu's focus is the connection between water, food and exercise — not out-featuring each specialist in its own field.

Story angles

  • New on Apple Watch: water, voice meal logging and GPS workouts with live heart rate on the wrist.
  • One app instead of three: why hydration, nutrition and training apps rarely talk to each other — and what changes when they do.
  • Coffee isn't water: counting 91 beverages by their real hydration value.
  • Built in Istanbul for 7 languages: including full right-to-left Arabic.
  • Privacy by design: voice is transcribed on the device and meal photos are never stored.
  • Giving back: 2% of Premium revenue funds water wells in Africa.

In Suu the run you log raises your water goal, the meal you eat triggers a digestion-water calculation, and the real hydration contribution of your coffee is subtracted. If you'd rather use one app than three — and hydration genuinely matters to you — Suu is the clear pick.

Frequently asked questions

Does Suu really do water tracking, calorie counting and exercise in one app?

Yes. Suu runs the three not as separate tabs but as a single system where each feeds the others: the exercise you log raises your daily water goal, the protein and sodium in your meal determine the extra water digestion requires, and every drink is counted with its real hydration contribution from a 91-beverage database.

How does photo calorie counting work?

You photograph your meal; Suu AI analyses it, extracts calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat, and builds your macros automatically. Your photo is not stored permanently. Photo analysis is a Premium feature; on the free plan you get 3 voice or text AI analyses per day.

How many sport activities does Suu support?

Around 50 different activities: running, walking, cycling, fitness, swimming, hiking, cardio and more. For running and walking you see distance, duration, pace, average speed and calories burned; for cycling you also get speed and elevation. After every workout your water goal updates automatically.

Is Suu free?

Core water and hydration tracking, 3 AI analyses per day and Apple Health / Google Health Connect sync are always free. Unlimited AI meal analysis, photo calorie counting, advanced nutrition analysis and premium statistics require Premium. Premium can be tried free for 3 days; the yearly plan is ₺1,200 with a 50% launch discount (normally ₺2,400), monthly is ₺149, and the 3-person family plan is ₺2,399 per year.

Which languages does Suu support?

The app is available in 7 languages: Türkçe, English, العربية (full right-to-left support), Deutsch, Italiano, Русский and हिन्दी.

Does Suu have an Apple Watch app?

Suu does not have its own Apple Watch app yet — it's coming soon. Today the workouts, steps and active energy your Apple Watch records flow into Suu through Apple Health and raise your water goal automatically. For hands-free logging from your wrist you can use the Siri command. Setup guide →

Is my data safe?

Your data belongs entirely to you. There is no ad tracking and data is not shared with third parties. With voice entry your speech is converted to text by your device's operating system — the audio is never sent to Suu's servers. Meal photos are not stored permanently after analysis. Privacy policy →

Why use Suu instead of MyFitnessPal or Yazio?

MyFitnessPal has a broader food database and Yazio has a stronger recipe library. What sets Suu apart is completeness: water tracking, photo calorie analysis and exercise tracking live in one app and are connected — your workout updates your water goal and your meal updates your digestion-water need.
